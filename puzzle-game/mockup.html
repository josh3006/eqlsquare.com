Trick Mind: clickable mockup

Play 4 levels from the 100-level set. Try the hint button, the free-coins ad and the shop to see how coins move. The art here is simple placeholder art; the final game uses illustrated cartoon scenes.

What each part of the screen does

  1. 1
    Coin counterShows the player's coins. Tapping it opens the shop. Real purchases go player → Apple/Google → your bank.
  2. 2
    QuestionUsually just text, but sometimes the text is part of the puzzle. Try level 9.
  3. 3
    Play areaPlayers tap, drag, type, and in the full game also shake, tilt or blow into the microphone.
  4. 4
    Hint buttonThree levels: nudge (10), strong hint (30), full solution (60). This is where most coins get spent.
  5. 5
    Free coinsWatch an ad for +20. Earns ad money from players who never buy anything.
