Bubble Bee Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026[cite: 1]
Published by EQL Square Services[cite: 1]

This policy applies to the current version of Bubble Bee for Android[cite: 1].

Information handled by the game

Bubble Bee does not collect or transmit personal information[cite: 1]. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs[cite: 1].

The game stores level progress, scores, banked pollen, and the selected character locally on your device[cite: 1]. The developer does not receive this information[cite: 1]. The app disables Android's standard app backup[cite: 1].

Purchases

The current version does not process purchases[cite: 1]. A one-time full-game unlock is planned[cite: 1]. This policy will be updated before payment functionality is released[cite: 1].

Sharing

The game does not send your information to the developer or share it with third parties[cite: 1].

Deleting local data

You can delete local game data through Android Settings by clearing the app's storage[cite: 1]. Uninstalling removes the installed app's local data; device backups may remain according to your backup settings[cite: 1].

Intended audience

Bubble Bee is intended for players aged 13 and above[cite: 1].

Changes

This policy will be updated when the game's data handling changes[cite: 1]. The date above indicates the latest revision[cite: 1].
